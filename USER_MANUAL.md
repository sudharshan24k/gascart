## 1. For Customers & General Users

### 1.1 Account Management

**Creating an Account (Sign Up)**
To purchase items, save cart details, or track orders, you need an account. 
1. Click on the **Sign Up** button located at the top right of the homepage.
2. Enter your Name, Email address, and a secure password.
3. You will receive an email confirmation. Click the link in the email to verify your account.

**Logging In & Logging Out**
1. Click **Log In** and enter your registered email and password.
2. To log out, go to your Profile icon on the top right and select **Logout**.

**Forgot Password**
If you forget your password, simply click "Forgot Password" on the login screen. Enter your email, and we will send you a secure link to reset it.

**Managing Your Profile**
Once logged in, click on your name or icon to access your **Profile**. Here you can:
- Update your personal information.
- View and manage your saved shipping and billing addresses.
- Access your entire order history.

### 1.2 Browsing & Purchasing Products

**Finding Products**
- **Search Bar:** Use the search bar at the top to type specific keywords (e.g., "Pressure Valve").
- **Categories:** Browse through clearly defined categories (e.g., Instruments, Pipes, Safety Gear) from the navigation menu.
- **Filters:** On the Product Listing page, you can filter products by price range, brand, technical specifications, and availability.

**Product Details**
Clicking on any product will open the **Product Detail** page. Here you will find:
- High-quality images of the product.
- Detailed technical specifications and descriptions.
- Availability status (In Stock / Out of Stock).
- Related products and documentation (e.g., manuals or safety data sheets).

**Comparing Products**
Not sure which product to choose? Click the **Compare** button next to multiple products. Navigate to the **Compare** page to view their features, prices, and specifications side-by-side to make an informed decision.

**Adding to Cart**
Once you find what you need, select the quantity and click **Add to Cart**. You can review all items you intend to purchase by clicking the **Cart** icon.

### 1.3 Checkout, Payments & Tax (GST)

**The Checkout Process**
1. Go to your Cart and click **Proceed to Checkout**.
2. **Shipping Details:** Select from your saved addresses or add a new shipping and billing address.
3. **Order Review:** Review the items in your cart.

**Understanding Taxes (GST)**
The platform automatically calculates an **18% GST (Goods and Services Tax)** on all taxable items. This is clearly shown in your order summary as a separate line item before you make a payment.

**Payment Integration (Razorpay)**
We use Razorpay, a highly secure and reliable payment gateway.
- Click **Pay Now** to proceed.
- A secure window will appear where you can pay using Credit/Debit Cards, UPI, Net Banking, or supported Digital Wallets.
- Do not close the window while the payment is processing.
- Upon successful payment, you will instantly be redirected to the **Order Success** page, and receive a confirmation email. 
- *Note: If a payment cancels or fails, you will be directed to a Payment Cancel screen where you can easily try again.*

### 1.4 Order Management & Tracking

**My Orders**
You can view a list of all your past and current purchases by going to your Profile and selecting **My Orders**. 

**Order Tracking**
Curious where your delivery is? Use the **Order Tracking** feature. Enter your Order ID, and you'll see real-time status updates (e.g., Processing, Shipped, In-Transit, Delivered).

### 1.5 Request for Quotation (RFQ)

For highly specialized bulk orders or custom equipment without a fixed price, you can submit an **RFQ**.
1. Navigate to the **Submit RFQ** page.
2. Fill out details regarding the specific products, quantities, custom requirements, and your timeline.
3. Submit the form. Our sales team will evaluate your request and send a detailed custom quotation to your account's **Enquiry List**.

### 1.6 Learning & Consultant Interaction

**Knowledge Hub (Learn)**
Access a vast repository of educational materials. Navigate to the **Learn** section to read detailed articles, whitepapers, and guides related to the Oil & Gas industry. Click on any article to open its **Article Detail** page.

**Connecting with Experts**
- Visit the **Consultants** page to view profiles of verified industry experts.
- Read through their **Expert Profiles** to see their specializations and experience.
- You can reach out directly to schedule consultations or ask specialized questions.

### 1.7 Careers at Stut Instruments
Looking to join our team? Visit the **Careers** page to view open job positions, read about our work culture, and submit job applications directly through the website.

## 2. For Consultants

We welcome industry experts to join our platform to offer consultancy services.

**Consultant Registration**
1. Navigate to the **Consultant Registration** page.
2. Fill out your professional details, including experience, certifications, niche expertise, and upload your resume/CV.
3. Your application goes to our Administrator team for approval.

**Consultant Dashboard**
Once approved, you get access to the **Consultant Dashboard**. Here, you can:
- Update your public-facing **Expert Profile**.
- View and respond to messages and inquiries from customers.
- Manage your consultation timeline.

## 3. For Vendors

If you are a manufacturer or supplier looking to list your products on the Stut Instruments supermarket, you must start by submitting a Vendor Enquiry.

**Vendor Enquiry Form**
1. Go to the **Vendor Enquiry** page.
2. Provide your company’s legal name, registration details, product catalog capabilities, and contact points.
3. Following submission, an administrative representative will contact you to set up your dedicated vendor portal and contracts.

## 4. For Administrators (Admin Dashboard)

The backend Admin Dashboard is the control center for the whole platform. It is restricted to authorized staff only.

### 4.1 Access & Overview
- **Admin Login:** Access via the dedicated `/admin` URL, requiring elevated admin credentials.
- **Admin Layout & Dashboard:** Upon logging in, you will be greeted by the primary Dashboard summarizing key metrics—Total Sales, Active Orders, Pending RFQs, and New Signups.

### 4.2 E-commerce Operations

**Category Management**
- Group products logically. You can add new categories, create sub-categories, or edit existing ones to help customers navigate the store easily.

**Products & Inventory**
- **Products:** Add new products complete with images (via Media Library), rich text descriptions, PDF manuals, prices, and specifications.
- **Inventory:** Keep track of stock. Set low-stock alerts and incrementally add stock as shipments arrive. 

**RFQ Configurator**
- Manage the settings and form fields associated with the Customer RFQ submission process.

### 4.3 Order & RFQ Management

**Orders**
- View every purchase made on the platform. 
- Update the status of an order (e.g., from "Processing" to "Shipped"), which automatically notifies the customer via the tracking page.

**RFQ Management**
- View custom quotation requests.
- Chat with the customer for clarifications.
- Send back official price quotes. Once the customer accepts the RFQ, an order is automatically generated.

### 4.4 User & Role Management

- **User Management:** View all customer accounts. Assist with basic account troubleshooting or suspend users violating terms of service.
- **Admin Management:** Invite new staff members and manage their dashboard access levels privileges.
- **Consultant Management:** Review new applications from the *Consultant Registration* page. Approve or reject their profiles. Support existing consultants with their dashboard queries viewing their interaction logs.
- **Consultant Inquiries:** A high-level view of all active conversations and bookings between customers and consultants.
- **Vendor Management:** Review Vendor Enquiries, onboard approved vendors, and manage their listing privileges.
- **Career Applications:** Review resumes and applications submitted through the Careers page. 

### 4.5 Content & Media Management

- **Media Library:** A centralized hub where all imagery, banners, and product photos are uploaded and hosted.
- **Document Center:** Manage vital downloadable documents like product manuals, legal compliance PDFs, or safety data sheets.
- **Knowledge Hub Management:** This acts as a blog/article editor. Draft, format, and publish posts directly to the Customer 'Learn' section.

### 4.6 System Administration

- **Audit Logs:** Security is paramount. The Audit Log tracks *every* action taken by any admin—who changed a product price, who authorized a refund, and who approved a vendor. This is crucial for accountability and troubleshooting.
