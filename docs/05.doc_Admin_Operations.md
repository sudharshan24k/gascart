# Admin Operations Guide

This document provides a guide for administrators on how to manage the Gascart platform using the Admin Dashboard. It covers core business workflows, from order fulfillment to vendor orchestration.

---

## 1. Dashboard Overview
The **Executive Dashboard** provides real-time matrix data on platform performance:
- **Gross Revenue**: Total volume of processed orders.
- **Order Queue**: Status breakdown of pending, active, and completed fulfillments.
- **Partner Ecosystem**: High-level count of active and pending vendors.

---

## 2. Order Fulfillment Workflow
Manage direct purchases and track the logistics lifecycle from the **Orders** page.

### Processing an Order
1. **Queue Review**: Filter orders by "Pending" to see new inbound purchases.
2. **Payment Verification**: Check the payment status (Paid vs. Partial). For partial payments, you can manually update balances if payments are received offline.
3. **Status Updates**: Advance the order through its lifecycle:
    - `Pending` → `Processing` (When the warehouse starts packing).
    - `Processing` → `Shipped` (When handed to the carrier).
    - `Shipped` → `Delivered` (Upon final confirmation).
4. **Logistics Tracking**:
    - Select an order to open the **Fulfillment Ops** panel.
    - Enter the **Carrier** (e.g., DHL, FedEx) and the **Tracking Serial/AWB**.
    - Clicking "Update Protocol" will sync the state and notify the user.

### Documentation & Exports
- **Invoices**: Generate individual PDF invoices for any order.
- **Bulk Export**: Select multiple orders to export their invoices as a single **ZIP archive** or download the entire dataset as a **CSV**.

---

## 3. Supply Chain Governance (Vendors)
Manage the partner ecosystem via the **Vendor Management** page.

### Onboarding New Partners
1. **Inbound Enquiries**: Click "Access Inquiries" to see companies requesting to join Gascart.
2. **KYC Review**: Review their company details, business type, and attached certifications.
3. **Authorization**: Click "Authorize & Notify" to convert an inquiry into a platform profile. This automatically generates a "Deep Cloak" (Hidden) profile for them.

### Active Partner Management
- **Visibility Control**: Set a partner to `Active` (Live on marketplace), `Inactive` (Suspended), or `Hidden` (Internal use only).
- **Partner Dossier**: Edit contact details, emails, and company metadata by clicking on any partner card.

---

## 4. Technical Enquiries (RFQs)
Industrial equipment often requires custom quotes. Manage these Engineering-led leads in **RFQ Management**.

### Handling Quote Requests
- **Itemized vs. Project**: Switch between single product requests and "Project Enquiries" where a user has requested quotes for multiple items at once.
- **Technical Specs**: View the specific technical payloads (e.g., pressure requirements, dimensions, material types) submitted by the customer.
- **Engineering Reports**: Export technical reports in **PDF format** for internal engineering review or **CSV** for CRM integration.

---

## 5. Catalog & Content Orchestration

### Product & Inventory
- **Asset Control**: Use the **Products** page to update specs, pricing, and visibility.
- **Stock Management**: Use the **Inventory** page for high-speed stock level updates across the entire catalog.
- **Media Library**: A centralized hub to manage and reuse product images, Avoiding duplicate uploads.

### Knowledge Hub
Manage articles, videos, and technical training content in the **Knowledge Hub**. This drives user engagement and provides "how-to" guides for complex equipment.

### Legal & Technical Documents
The **Document Center** is used to manage PDFs for:
- Terms & Conditions / Privacy Policies.
- Technical Datasheets.
- Vendor Agreements.

---

## 6. Security & Human Resources

### Audit Logs
Transparency is maintained through the **Audit Logs**. Every administrative action—including status changes, stock updates, and vendor approvals—is recorded with a timestamp and the acting admin's identity.

### Career Applications
Review resumes and applications submitted through the frontend "Careers" page. Admins can update application statuses and securely access resume documents stored in private Supabase buckets.
